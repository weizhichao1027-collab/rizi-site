怎么添加日子？

How do I add a day?

打开 App，点「添加日子」，填写名称并选择日期，然后保存。只需这两步。空列表时首页有入口，之后也可以用右上角加号。

Open the app, tap Add a day, enter a name, pick a date, then save. That is the whole flow. The empty list has a button; later you can use the plus control.

「就是今天 / 还剩 N 天 / 已过 N 天」怎么算？

How are Today, N days left, and N days ago counted?

按自然日计算，不按小时。列表、详情、主屏幕和锁屏小组件走同一套口径，保持一致。

By calendar day, not by the hour. The list, detail screen, Home Screen widgets, and Lock Screen widgets share the same count.

小组件天数怎么和 App 里对不上？

Why doesn’t the widget match the app?

在主屏幕或锁屏长按空白处，点加号或「自定」，搜索「日子」或 Days，选一个尺寸后添加。可以让它自动跟最近的日子，也可以长按小组件后指定某一条。添加后应与详情一致。若刚改过日期，稍等刷新，或离开桌面再回来看。

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap plus or Customize, search for Days, then add a size. It can follow the nearest day, or you can pin one after a long press. It should match the detail count. After you change a date, wait for a refresh, or leave and return to the Home Screen.

主题只能预览不能用？

I can preview a theme but not use it?

免费主题（考试冲刺、生日派对）点「使用」即可。带锁的主题需要解锁 Pro。点卡片只预览，不会改掉当前主题；点「使用」才会写入。若尚未购买，点「使用」会进入购买。

Free themes (Exam Sprint, Birthday Party) can be used immediately. Locked themes need Pro. Tapping a card only previews it. Tap Use to apply it. If Pro is not unlocked, Use opens purchase.

购买没成功，或提示失败？

The purchase failed or was cancelled?

确认网络正常，用你的 Apple ID 完成系统购买框。若点了取消，会提示「已取消」。真正失败可以再试一次。仍不行时：在 App Store 退出后重新登录，或稍后再试。

Check the network and finish the Apple ID sheet. Cancel shows Cancelled. A real failure can be retried. If it still fails, sign out and back into the App Store, or try later.

换机或重装后主题没了？

Themes disappeared after a new phone or reinstall?

打开日子，进入设置或 Pro 入口，点「恢复购买」。需要使用购买时的同一个 Apple ID。日子本身只保存在这台设备上，换机不会自动带过来；请自己重新添加日子。Pro 解锁可以恢复。

Open Days, go to Settings or the Pro sheet, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Days themselves stay on that device and will not migrate automatically; add them again. The Pro unlock can be restored.

会不会要订阅？

Is this a subscription?

不会。Pro 是一次买断（非消耗型），不是订阅。中文商店价为 ¥6；其他地区显示当地价格。

No. Pro is a one-time unlock (non-consumable), not a subscription. The price is local in the App Store.

收不收集我的数据？要日历权限吗？

Do you collect my data? Do I need calendar access?

不收集个人数据。日子与主题保存在本机，不需要相机、相册或系统日历权限。详见隐私政策。

We do not collect personal data. Days and themes stay on device. Camera, photo library, and system calendar access are not required. See the privacy policy.
