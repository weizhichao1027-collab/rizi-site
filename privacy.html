Days runs on your device and does not require an account. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

我们收集哪些数据

Data we collect

我们不收集个人数据。App 不接入用于识别你身份的分析、广告或崩溃上报 SDK。

We do not collect personal data. The app does not include analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs that identify you.

仅保存在你设备上的内容

Stored only on your device

  • 你添加的日子（名称、日期）
  • 主题与显示偏好
  • 是否已解锁 Pro（通过 Apple 查询购买状态）
  • Days you add (name and date)
  • Theme and display preferences
  • Whether Pro is unlocked (queried from Apple)

这些保存在设备本地（含小组件所用的 App Group）。卸载 App 后会删除。

This stays on device, including the App Group used by widgets, and is deleted when you delete the app.

权限

Permissions

日子不需要相机、相册、麦克风、定位、通讯录、系统日历或追踪权限。桌面与锁屏小组件仅读取本机已保存的日子数据。

Days does not need camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, system calendar, or tracking access. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets only read days already saved on this device.

购买

Purchases

可选的 Pro 解锁由 Apple App 内购买处理。付款信息由 Apple 处理，我们收不到你的银行卡信息。我们仅从 Apple 获知该非消耗型商品是否已购买。Pro 为一次买断，不是订阅。

Optional Pro unlocks are handled by Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment; we never receive your card details. We only learn from Apple whether the non-consumable product was purchased. Pro is a one-time unlock, not a subscription.

儿童

Children

App 分级为 4+。我们不以儿童为对象收集数据，也不故意收集任何人的个人数据。

The app is rated 4+. We do not target children and do not knowingly collect anyone’s personal data.

第三方

Third parties

除 Apple（App Store / StoreKit）外，App 不向第三方发送你的数据。

Except Apple (App Store / StoreKit), the app does not send your data to third parties.

本网站

This website

本页说明的是「日子」iOS App。网站为静态页面，托管于 GitHub Pages；访问时托管方可能记录常规服务器日志。本站用 localStorage 记住你选择的语言（中 / EN），不用于追踪，可在浏览器中清除。

This policy covers the Days iOS app. The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages; the host may keep ordinary server logs. The site stores your language choice (Chinese / EN) in localStorage. It is not used for tracking and can be cleared in the browser.

政策变更

Changes

若本政策变更，我们会更新上方日期，并在本页面发布新版本。

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version on this page.
